# Privacy Policy & Data Deletion for Val Shop

Val Shop is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains how we handle your data and how you can request its deletion.

## 1. Data We Collect
Val Shop collects the following information from your Riot Games account during the login process:
* **User Identifiers**: Your Riot PUUID.
* **Personal Information**: Your Game Name and Tagline.
* **Account Info**: Your Player Level and Title.
* **Authentication Tokens**: Access tokens and Entitlements tokens required to fetch your store data.

## 2. Data Usage & Storage
* **Local Storage**: All data collected is stored **locally** on your mobile device using encrypted or standard internal storage (SharedPreferences).
* **No Server Storage**: Val Shop **does not** have a backend server. We never upload your account details, passwords, or tokens to our own servers or any third-party other than Riot Games' official APIs.

## 3. Data Deletion (User Control)
You have full control over your data. You can delete all stored information using any of the following methods:

### Within the App:
1. **Logout**: Go to the **Settings** menu and tap the **Logout icon** next to your account. This will immediately delete your tokens and profile data from the device.
2. **Clear Cache**: Tap the **Clear Store Cache** option in Settings to remove all cached storefront data.

### Outside the App:
1. **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the Val Shop app from your Android device will automatically delete all local files and data associated with the application.
